---
name: Pipeline decision gate
description: Why Pipeline activation deliberately differs from ordinary topic creation and does not reserve delivery capacity.
---

Pipeline is a topic lifecycle stage, not a separate backlog or duplicated topic.

**Why:** The user wants proposals to retain their identity, description, routing and history when activated.

**How to apply:** Activation always enters standard department Head/Deputy validation, even if the creator would normally auto-approve an ordinary topic in their own scope. Preserve ordinary topic auto-approval and existing break-glass rules unchanged.

Pipeline must not reserve collaborators' delivery capacity. Proposal-level estimated effort is separate from milestone planning.

**Why:** Undecided proposals must not reduce people's available hours before a delivery decision.

**How to apply:** Do not allow Pipeline milestone allocations; exclude Pipeline and Not pursued from occupancy and active delivery calendars. Retain high-level topic collaboration separately from booked work.

Waiting-for categories are decision, budget, vendor and partner input. A review date supports reminders. Not pursued is an exit requiring a reason and retained history.

**Why:** The user wants proposals reviewed rather than forgotten and a way to stop pursuing them rather than letting the backlog grow indefinitely.

**How to apply:** Use existing digest settings for review reminders, with the creator and configured reviewers as recipients. Never classify these as urgent security/break-glass mail.
