---
name: Project priority and overbooking
description: User's business rule for milestone overbooking and BAU priority conflicts.
---

Milestone planning may exceed a member's combined 100% capacity. Show negative availability in red rather than blocking the allocation. BAU allocations themselves must remain capped at 100%.

**Why:** The user says overbooking is a common issue and projects take priority over daily BAU; members and management need to see that BAU may not be fully covered.

**How to apply:** Show date-dependent availability when assigning milestone work, accounting for BAU and overlapping commitments. Do not automatically reduce stored BAU allocations to hide the conflict.
