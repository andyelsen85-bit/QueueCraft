---
name: Contract-hour estimates
description: User-approved assumptions for BAU weekly hours and milestone total-hour estimates.
---

Leave a member’s contracted weekly hours unset until entered. Never silently assume a 40-hour contract.

Estimate milestone hours using Monday–Friday, including both endpoint dates when they are weekdays. Public holidays still count. Spread the contracted weekly hours evenly across five working days.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose weekday-based milestone estimates and unset contracts over calendar-day estimates and a 40-hour default.

**How to apply:** Use these assumptions consistently in live percentage labels, saved allocation summaries, and BAU exports. Missing contracts or missing dates must remain unknown, not become fabricated estimates.
