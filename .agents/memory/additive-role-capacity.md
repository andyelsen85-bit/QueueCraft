---
name: Additive role capacity
description: How role-level planning totals should treat shared members and occupancy from other roles.
---

Role capacity is additive: every active person contributes 100% capacity, and the displayed occupied total is the sum of each included person's full occupancy. Do not replace this with an average normalized to 100%. Count each person once within a role, including the lead and deputy, even if the person is also assigned to another role. A person's commitments from all roles contribute to their occupancy in every role where they appear.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose additive totals to see the actual collective capacity available for role-driven projects. New work assigned through any role must reduce that person's availability everywhere they are counted.

**How to apply:** In role reports and planning views, show total capacity as 100% per active member, sum global member loads, and derive signed free capacity from the difference. Distinguish usable per-person availability and individual overcommitment when one person's load exceeds 100%; do not let role filtering remove the person's commitments from other roles.
