---
name: Topic routing and approval
description: Unassigned pending topics and preserving approval when departments change.
---

Topics may be created without a department or role. Keep them pending validation until an existing department and a matching active role are defined. Standard and break-glass approval both require complete routing; do not invent a placeholder department or infer one from the creator.

Changing a previously approved topic's department must preserve its status and existing approval history, rather than returning it to pending validation.

**Why:** The user requested unassigned topic creation and explicitly selected “Keep existing approval” for department changes.

**How to apply:** Keep existing topic-management permissions for transfers, allow service authorities to review unassigned pending topics, and keep approval scoped to the selected department (or use the existing break-glass authorization).
