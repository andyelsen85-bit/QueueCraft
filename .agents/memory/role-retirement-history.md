---
name: Role retirement and topic history
description: Deleting a role while preserving the attribution of finished work.
---

Deleting a role removes it from active choices but retains its identity for historical topics. Require unfinished topics (including Open and In Progress) to be moved to another role first. Completed and closed topics stay attributed to the original role; rejected topics are also treated as historical.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants to remove roles without transferring finished work or losing its history. Physically deleting a referenced role would break those historical links.

**How to apply:** Keep the old role resolvable for historical display, never offer it for new assignments, and prevent reopening an old topic under a retired role without choosing an active one.
