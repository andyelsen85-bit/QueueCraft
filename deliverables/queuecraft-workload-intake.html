Your current workload and active topics

Please describe the work you are actually doing now so we can establish a useful starting point in QueueCraft. This is a planning snapshot, not a performance assessment or a request to justify every hour.

Scope: Report your typical workload this week and the active topics you expect to work on over the next four weeks. Include known changes in workload or dates. Do not include completed work or ideas that have not yet been agreed as active work; mention likely upcoming work separately.

How to estimate your occupancy

  • Use your normal working week as 100%. If you work part-time, 100% means your own contracted week, not someone else's full-time week. As a rough guide, one day out of a five-day week is 20%.
  • Business as usual (BAU) is recurring work such as support, operations, routine meetings, administration and regular service duties. Name each activity and estimate its share of your normal week. The BAU entries should add up to no more than 100%.
  • Active topics are distinct pieces of work with an outcome and a likely start/end period. For each, estimate the percentage of your normal week you currently spend on it. Give your best estimate rather than waiting for precise timesheets.
  • Count work once. If a milestone is already included in a topic's percentage, do not add its effort again. QueueCraft adds milestone workload on top of topic allocation when it is entered separately.
  • Be honest if you are over capacity. BAU + topics + any separately counted milestones may be above 100%. Do not reduce estimates just to make the total look neat; flag overload and any dates when it will change.

Example: BAU: support 20% + recurring meetings 15% = 35%. Active topics: Service migration 30% + Reporting update 20% = 50%. Total occupancy: 85%; available: 15%. If a migration milestone is already within the 30%, it is not another allocation.

What to tell us about each active topic

Give a clear title, a one-sentence description of the intended outcome, your role, your typical weekly allocation, an approximate start and expected finish date, and the people involved if known. You may also give an estimate of total effort in hours, priority or a deadline if you have one. Mark uncertain estimates as “approximate”; name blockers or decisions that may change your dates.

Important: A topic's allocation in QueueCraft applies across its recorded start–finish period. If your workload rises or falls substantially during that period, explain when and why so the person entering the data can model it appropriately. If work belongs to your recurring BAU rather than a time-bounded topic, list it as BAU instead.

Before you send your answer

  • Have you included all recurring work, not only project work?
  • Are the percentages based on your normal working week and free of double counting?
  • Have you named each topic, estimated dates and flagged uncertainty or overload?
